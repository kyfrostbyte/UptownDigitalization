Terms and Conditions

Service Sessions

The Laser Hair Removal package for all service areas includes 8 treatments. The pricing of your package is based on the purchase price of 4 individual treatments with 4 additional treatments added to your package complimentary, with no additional charge.

The sessions for all service areas must be scheduled at least 8 weeks apart. You must complete your 8 treatments within twenty four (24) months of the date of this invoice signed by you. Any of your treatments not completed within this twenty four (24) months period will be forfeited and no refund will be issued.

Guests with a Lifetime Membership will be eligible to have additional treatment at a fixed rate that will be specified in this agreement. These fees are fixed based on the treatment area and may not be raised.

Additional Services and Policies

Any additional services or service areas will require a new invoice charged at our then-current fees.

Uptown reserves the right to charge a $25 fee for appointments cancelled or rescheduled without 24 hour notice prior to treatment time.

Before any services are rendered by us, you will sign the Informed Consent and Authorization. You agree that we may cancel a service if the service requirements (as defined in the Informed Consent and Authorization) are not met to our satisfaction. You understand that service requirements must be followed for each and every service date and in between services.

Refunds

There are no refunds for completed treatments. If a refund is desired, it is understood the package will revert to individual treatment pricing as the package option with complimentary treatments will be forfeited. Each of the first 4 treatments can be refunded at 25%, per treatment, of the total package cost for any treatment, #1-4, that have not been completed. Once treatment #4 is completed, no additional refunds are available as the remaining 4 treatments are given complimentary and at no cost. A Lifetime Membership is only available with the purchase of a full package, so if any portion of a package is refunded, it is understood that the Lifetime Membership has been forfeited.
