Informed Consent and Authorization

Laser Hair Reduction Procedure

Overall Consent

I consent to having Uptown Laser's trained staff perform the Laser Hair Reduction (LHR) procedure. I acknowledge having been informed that this cosmetic procedure will be provided with a Candela Alexandrite Laser and/or a Candela GentleYAG laser. These lasers are manufactured by a third party, Candela Corporation. I understand that the LHR Services must be repeated several times before satisfactory results are achieved although there is no warranty or guaranty that all hair will be permanently removed from the Treatment Area. I understand that gray, white, blonde, or red hair color will not respond to LHR. I understand that a different provider may provide treatments at each session.

Physician Communication

I understand that this is a delegated medical procedure and that the physician medical director may not be on site at the time of my consultation and or treatments. The physician's identity and contact information have been made readily available to me. Treatment notes in medical records will not be shared with individuals or outside medical facilities (including, but not limited to, my primary care physician) without my expressed and written request.

Appointments

I will endeavor to arrive at least 5 minutes before my scheduled appointment. In the event I need to make any changes to my appointment, I will inform provider at least 24 hours prior.

Photographing

I consent to the photographing of this procedure to document treatment results, for medical reasons, or for use in marketing materials provided such photos do not reveal my identity.

Pre and Post Care Instructions

I acknowledge my obligation to strictly comply with all of the Treatment Requirements identified below in this Agreement. I agree that provider may refuse treatment if the Treatment Requirements are not met to the provider's satisfaction with respect to each and every session. I understand that I must notify provider if I have not followed the Treatment Requirements noted below:

(a) Sun Exposure. I agree to refrain from all forms of sun exposure, tanning sprays, and UV lights, for at least four (4) weeks prior to and four (4) weeks after each session or as directed by your Provider.
(b) Anesthetic Cream. I agree to follow all anesthetic cream instructions.
(c) Hair Removal. I agree that you will not wax, tweeze, or use hair removal creams during the Treatment Schedule. I agree to shave the Treatment Area within twenty-four hours (24) prior to each session, unless otherwise instructed by laser treatment provider.
(d) Medications. I agree to refrain from the use of skin creams and medications that may cause skin sensitivity for at least two (2) weeks prior to each session.
(e) Health Conditions. I understand that I will need to notify provider promptly if any health conditions have changed during the Treatment Schedule. These include, but are not limited to pregnancy, polycystic ovarian syndrome (PCOS), and other immune deficiency, hormonal, and metabolic disorders.

Treatment Protocols

I understand that the LHR Services will involve using laser beams at an energy dosage that is relative to skin color, hair color, and texture. I also understand that a light cortisone-type ointment, aloe, gel, and/or ice packs may be administered to the Treatment Area. I understand that the total duration of each treatment will vary based on the size of the treatment area. I understand that the alternatives to laser hair removal include shaving, waxing, and electrolysis.

Risks

I understand that the LHR Services may involve the following risks or discomforts:

  • Temporary pain, burning, or a stinging sensation at or near the Treatment Area
  • Redness and/or swelling at or near the Treatment Area
  • Ulceration and/or infection at or near the Treatment Area
  • Pigmentary (color) changes at the Treatment Area, including decrease in skin color (hypopigmentation or lightening) and/or increase in skin color (hyperpigmentation or darkening)
  • Scar formation in the Treatment Area
  • Laser-induced burns or blistering in the Treatment Area or surrounding tissue
  • Poor cosmetic outcome and/or recurrence of, increase of, or stimulation of hair growth in Treatment Area
  • Inadvertent damage or removal of moles, beauty marks, or tattoos
  • Eye injury
  • Temporary increase of acne lesions or folliculitis in the treatment area

Conditions Not Treated

I understand that the following health conditions will not be treated by the laser treatment providers: melanoma, AIDS, nor will treatment occur over active fungal, bacterial or viral rashes or lesions (e.g., genital warts). I acknowledge and represent that I am not experiencing any of the above conditions. I agree that, if I develop any of the above conditions at any time that I will immediately notify the laser treatment provider of the condition. I understand the provider may elect to postpone my treatment if I have had a recent chemical peel, laser peel, or other cosmetic procedure, or any other temporary condition of my skin that the provider deems should resolve prior to your next laser treatment.

Medical Information

I consent to the provider to use my medical information for purposes of informing me of, or marketing to me, additional goods or services that provider may offer from time to time. I consent to the provider to use or disclose my medical information as needed for payment or healthcare operations purposes. For example, we may disclose your information to billing companies, consultants, and other professionals as needed for our day-to-day business activities.

I have read this Agreement in full, and understand all of the provisions in this Agreement, and have been given the opportunity to ask questions regarding the LHR Services.
